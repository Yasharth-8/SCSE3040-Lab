__init__.py: Defines the delivery package and exposes its main functions for convenient importing.

data.py: Loads the delivery dataset and splits it into training and testing data.

features.py: Provides functions for checking and describing individual delivery orders.

model.py: Handles training, evaluating, saving, and loading the machine-learning model.

validate.py: Checks whether a delivery order satisfies all required validation rules.

train.py: Runs the training pipeline from the command line and saves the trained model.

predict.py: Loads the saved model and generates a prediction for a delivery order.
